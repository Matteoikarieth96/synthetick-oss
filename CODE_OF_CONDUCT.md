# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a place where people of any background can learn, build and disagree without fear.
This text is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1; see Attribution at the end.

## Our standard

Things that make the community better:
- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Giving and accepting feedback on the work, not the person.
- Owning mistakes, apologising, and learning from them.
- Putting the health of the whole community ahead of winning an argument.

Things that are not acceptable:
- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexualised language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or publishing someone's private information.
- Pushing a coin, a token or a trade in the issue tracker. This is a research project, not a place to promote assets.
- Anything else that would be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may ban people who keep breaking them.
Report a problem by opening a private report through the repository's **Security** tab (choose "Report a vulnerability" and mark it as a conduct report) or by contacting a maintainer on GitHub.
Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is protected.

Consequences follow this ladder: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, then a permanent ban.
