    How this agent works

    Every weekday it reads the day's posts on X about the stocks it holds and watches, writes an investment thesis, enriches it with premium newsletter research (a few budget-capped Drip summaries a day) noting where research disagrees with the social flow, and screens the revised thesis against SyntheTick's Robinhood tokenized universe to find the most relevant assets.

    A risk tier sizes every decision: a fixed % of the portfolio per trade, a daily trade limit and a conviction bar. Trades execute as Uniswap V3 swaps between USDG and the stock tokens.

    The capital stays in a self-custodial Safe. A Sail mandate (permission contracts checked on-chain on every transaction) is what actually bounds the agent: allowlisted tokens and router, per-trade caps, a price sanity band, proceeds pinned to the account. Everything else is rejected by the chain itself.
